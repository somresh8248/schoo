What are the school Admissions requirements?

Admissions at Academic Heights Public School are open for all grade levels. Interested parents can fill out the online Admissions form or visit the school campus to collect the prospectus and Admissions kit.

What curriculum does Academic Heights Public School follow?

We follow a holistic, learner-centric curriculum aligned with modern educational standards that emphasizes academic excellence, co-curricular learning, sports, and character development.

What extra-curricular activities are available for students?

Our school offers a wide variety of activities including music, dance, visual arts, robotics, public speaking, cricket, basketball, and various student clubs.

How can parents track student progress?

Parents receive regular performance reports through our digital portal and interactive parent-teacher meetings organized every term.

Academic Heights Public School is dedicated to nurturing curiosity, creativity, and leadership in every student, preparing them for academic success and life.
