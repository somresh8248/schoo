Welcome to Academic Heights Public School - Varthur

Where Every Child Learns, Grows, and Shines

At Academic Heights Public School (AHPS) Varthur, we nurture confident learners and global citizens through a safe, stimulating curriculum. Our campus features modern smart classrooms, robotics training, expansive sports grounds, and a swimming pool.

We actively foster creativity through events like our Art Contest celebrations, giving students opportunities to excel academically and express their artistic talents.
