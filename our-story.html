Welcome to AHPS Varthur

Nurturing Minds, Preparing for Life

Academic Heights Public School was established with the vision of providing quality education that prepares children not only for academic success but also for life.

We strive to create an environment where curiosity is encouraged, creativity is celebrated, and every learner is empowered to achieve their fullest potential. Our educational philosophy focuses on developing confident communicators, critical thinkers, compassionate leaders, and responsible citizens.

Academic Heights Public School is dedicated to nurturing curiosity, creativity, and leadership in every student, preparing them for academic success and life.
