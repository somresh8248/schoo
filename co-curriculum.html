CO-CURRICULAR PROGRAMMES

Children develop confidence beyond the classroom through a wide array of activities designed to nurture talent, creativity, physical wellness, and leadership.

OUR CO-CURRICULAR OFFERINGS

Empowering every child to discover their unique interests, sharpen their skills, and shine with self-assurance.

01

Sports

Building endurance, agility, teamwork, and sportsmanship through outdoor and indoor athletic disciplines.

02

Yoga

Promoting physical flexibility, mental focus, stress management, and holistic bodily awareness.

03

Chess

Fostering strategic foresight, critical thinking, problem-solving, and patience in intellectual gaming.

Music

Cultivating auditory expression, vocal training, rhythm, and instrumental harmony for musical appreciation.

05

Developing graceful posture, cultural heritage appreciation, rhythm, and expressive stage performance.

06

Unleashing visual imagination, color theory, sketch mastery, and creative fine-art techniques.

Enhancing fine motor control, spatial reasoning, model making, origami, and artisanal design skills.

08

Nurturing theatrical presence, emotional empathy, storytelling, and communicative confidence.

09

Empowering persuasive debate, eloquent articulation, stage confidence, and public presentation skills.

STEM Clubs

Inspiring scientific curiosity, robotics experiments, coding fundamentals, and innovative problem-solving.

Environmental Activities

12

Community Service

Building compassionate leadership, social empathy, outreach initiatives, and active civic awareness.
