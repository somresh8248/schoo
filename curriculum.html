Foundation & Competitive Edge

Integrated Learning with Aakash Institute

At Academic Heights Public School (AHPS), we are proud to partner with Aakash Institute – Gunjur, bringing together the excellence of school education and India's trusted foundation coaching.

This integrated program is designed for Grades VII & VIII, providing structured preparation for Olympiads, NEET, and IIT-JEE Foundation alongside the regular school curriculum.

With expert guidance, concept-based learning, and a well-planned academic approach, students develop stronger analytical skills, problem-solving abilities, and the confidence to excel in competitive examinations. This collaboration ensures that every learner receives a future-ready education without compromising the joy of school life.

Key Pillars of Growth

Core Focus Areas

Language Development

Developing listening, speaking, reading, and early writing skills through storytelling, phonics, vocabulary games, and expressive communication.

Numeracy Skills

Building foundational mathematical concepts including number sense, counting, patterns, shapes, spatial awareness, and logical thinking.

Social & Emotional Development

Fostering self-confidence, empathy, sharing, cooperation, emotion management, and building healthy peer relationships in a supportive environment.

Gross & Fine Motor Skills

Enhancing physical coordination, balance, agility, hand-eye control through outdoor play, sensory activities, and fine manipulation.

Creativity & Imagination

Encouraging artistic expression, imaginative role-play, music, dance, rhythm, drama, and open-ended creative exploration.

Environmental Awareness

Inspiring natural curiosity about environment, animals, weather, healthy living habits, and caring for the world around us.
