Safety & Reliability

SCHOOL TRANSPORT SERVICES

Safe and reliable transport services are available on selected routes.

Key Features

GPS Tracking

Real-time vehicle tracking enabled on all fleet buses for live location monitoring and parent peace of mind.

Trained Drivers

Professional, certified, and thoroughly background-verified drivers trained in defensive driving techniques.

Lady Attendants

Dedicated female attendants onboard every route for younger children to ensure comfort, safety, and personal care.

Regular Safety Checks

Strict routine maintenance audits, speed governors, emergency exit drills, first-aid kits, and fire extinguishers.

Need Route Information or Bus Seat Allocation?

Contact our Transport Desk for route maps, timing schedules, and fee details.
