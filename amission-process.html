Step-by-Step Guide

Admissions Process
01

1. Submit Enquiry Form

Submit the Admissions Enquiry Form online or at the school reception to express interest.

02

2. Visit the Campus

Take a guided walkthrough of our modern campus, laboratories, and learning facilities.

03

3. Parent & Child Interaction

An informal interaction with our academic counselors and leadership team.

04

4. Document Verification

Verification of mandatory student identity, age proof, and previous academic records.

05

5. Confirmation of Admissions

Official grant and confirmation of seat allocation by the school Admissionss authority.

06

6. Fee Payment

Completion of fee deposit to secure your child's enrollment for the academic session.
